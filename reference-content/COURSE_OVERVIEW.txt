================================================================================
                        PROGRAM DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

The HR Verse Professional Human Resource Management Program is a comprehensive, 
industry-aligned learning experience designed to equip participants with the 
knowledge, skills, and competencies required to excel in modern Human Resource 
Management. This program bridges the gap between academic theory and practical 
application, offering a structured curriculum that covers the full spectrum of 
HR functions—from strategic workforce planning and talent acquisition to 
employee relations, compensation design, and HR technology.

Developed in consultation with senior HR practitioners, organizational 
psychologists, and learning design experts, this program reflects current best 
practices, emerging trends, and regulatory frameworks that shape the HR 
profession today. Whether you are an aspiring HR professional, a manager 
seeking to strengthen your people-management capabilities, or an experienced 
practitioner pursuing formal certification, HR Verse provides the depth and 
rigor necessary to advance your career.

================================================================================
                        PROGRAM OBJECTIVES
================================================================================

Upon successful completion of this program, participants will be able to:

1.  STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT
    - Articulate the strategic role of Human Resource Management in achieving 
      organizational objectives.
    - Develop HR strategies that align with business goals, market conditions, 
      and organizational culture.

2.  TALENT MANAGEMENT
    - Design and implement effective recruitment, selection, and onboarding 
      processes that attract and retain top talent.
    - Build workforce plans that anticipate future talent needs and address 
      skill gaps.

3.  COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
    - Construct equitable and competitive compensation structures using 
      established job evaluation and market-pricing methodologies.
    - Administer employee benefits programs that enhance engagement and 
      support organizational competitiveness.

4.  PERFORMANCE & DEVELOPMENT
    - Implement performance management systems that drive accountability, 
      continuous improvement, and employee growth.
    - Design training and development programs grounded in adult learning 
      theory and aligned with organizational competency frameworks.

5.  EMPLOYEE RELATIONS & COMPLIANCE
    - Navigate employment law, labor relations, and regulatory compliance 
      with confidence and ethical integrity.
    - Foster positive employee relations through effective communication, 
      conflict resolution, and grievance management.

6.  HR ANALYTICS & TECHNOLOGY
    - Leverage HR information systems (HRIS), data analytics, and emerging 
      technologies to inform evidence-based HR decision-making.
    - Evaluate and implement digital HR solutions that improve operational 
      efficiency and employee experience.

7.  ORGANIZATIONAL DEVELOPMENT
    - Apply change management principles to lead organizational 
      transformation initiatives.
    - Cultivate inclusive workplace cultures that promote diversity, equity, 
      and belonging.

8.  PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE
    - Demonstrate ethical judgment, professional accountability, and 
      continuous learning as core tenets of HR practice.
    - Communicate HR insights and recommendations effectively to diverse 
      stakeholder audiences.

================================================================================
                         TARGET AUDIENCE
================================================================================

This program is designed for:

•   ASPIRING HR PROFESSIONALS
    Individuals seeking to enter the HR field who want a solid foundation in 
    HR principles, practices, and professional standards before pursuing 
    entry-level HR roles.

•   HR GENERALISTS & SPECIALISTS
    Practicing HR professionals who wish to broaden their knowledge across 
    multiple HR domains, update their skills with current best practices, or 
    prepare for professional certification examinations.

•   PEOPLE MANAGERS & TEAM LEADERS
    Line managers, supervisors, and team leaders who are responsible for 
    people management functions (hiring, performance reviews, coaching) and 
    want to strengthen their HR competencies.

•   BUSINESS OWNERS & ENTREPRENEURS
    Small business owners and startup founders who manage HR functions 
    in-house and need practical knowledge to build effective people 
    management systems.

•   CAREER CHANGERS
    Professionals transitioning from other disciplines (finance, operations, 
    marketing) into HR roles who require a structured learning pathway.

PREREQUISITES:
    - No formal HR experience is required.
    - A bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience is recommended 
      but not mandatory.
    - Basic proficiency in English (reading, writing, and comprehension).
    - Access to a computer with reliable internet connectivity.

================================================================================
                       LEARNING OUTCOMES
================================================================================

By the end of this program, successful participants will demonstrate 
competency in the following areas:

KNOWLEDGE OUTCOMES (Know):
    K1. Explain the evolution, scope, and strategic importance of HRM in 
        contemporary organizations.
    K2. Describe the legal and regulatory frameworks governing employment 
        relationships in major jurisdictions.
    K3. Identify the components of total rewards systems, including base pay, 
        variable pay, and non-monetary benefits.
    K4. Outline the principles of organizational behavior, motivation theory, 
        and their application to HR practice.
    K5. Discuss emerging trends in HR technology, people analytics, and the 
        future of work.

SKILL OUTCOMES (Do):
    S1. Conduct job analysis and produce accurate job descriptions and 
        person specifications.
    S2. Design structured interview protocols and assessment center 
        exercises for candidate selection.
    S3. Build compensation structures using point-factor job evaluation 
        and salary benchmarking techniques.
    S4. Develop performance management frameworks incorporating goal 
        setting, continuous feedback, and calibration processes.
    S5. Create training programs using the ADDIE instructional design model.
    S6. Analyze HR metrics and present data-driven recommendations to 
        leadership stakeholders.

ATTITUDINAL OUTCOMES (Be):
    A1. Demonstrate ethical awareness and integrity in all HR 
        decision-making processes.
    A2. Embrace diversity, equity, and inclusion as foundational values in 
        people management.
    A3. Exhibit a commitment to continuous professional development and 
        lifelong learning.
    A4. Adopt a strategic, business-partner mindset in approaching HR 
        challenges.

================================================================================
                      PROGRAM STRUCTURE (8 WEEKS)
================================================================================

The program is organized into eight (8) thematic weeks, each containing 
two to three modules, supplementary readings, practical exercises, and 
assessments.

WEEK 1: FOUNDATIONS OF HRM & TALENT ACQUISITION
    Module 01: Introduction to Human Resource Management
    Module 02: HR Planning & Job Analysis
    Module 03: Recruitment & Selection
    Quiz: Week 1 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 2: REWARDING & DEVELOPING PEOPLE
    Module 04: Compensation & Benefits Management
    Module 05: Performance Management Systems
    Module 06: Training & Development
    Quiz: Week 2 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Assessment 1 Due: HR Strategy Development
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 3: EMPLOYEE RELATIONS & EMPLOYMENT LAW
    Module 07: Employee Relations & Engagement
    Module 08: Employment Law & Regulatory Compliance
    Quiz: Week 3 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 4: ORGANIZATIONAL BEHAVIOR & CULTURE
    Module 09: Organizational Behavior Fundamentals
    Module 10: Workplace Culture, Diversity & Inclusion
    Quiz: Week 4 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Assessment 2 Due: Case Study Analysis
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 5: HR ANALYTICS & TECHNOLOGY
    Module 11: HR Information Systems (HRIS)
    Module 12: People Analytics & Data-Driven HR
    Quiz: Week 5 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 6: CHANGE MANAGEMENT & ORGANIZATIONAL DEVELOPMENT
    Module 13: Change Management Principles
    Module 14: Organizational Development & Transformation
    Quiz: Week 6 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Project Part 1 Due: HR Audit
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 7: STRATEGIC HR & GLOBAL HRM
    Module 15: Strategic Human Resource Management
    Module 16: Global & International HRM
    Quiz: Week 7 Knowledge Check (20 Questions)
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

WEEK 8: CAPSTONE & CERTIFICATION
    Module 17: The Future of Work & Emerging HR Trends
    Module 18: HR Professional Ethics & Career Development
    Project Part 2 Due: HR Digital Transformation Plan
    Final Examination
    Estimated Effort: 15 hours

TOTAL PROGRAM EFFORT: Approximately 120 hours

================================================================================
                      ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK
================================================================================

Participants are assessed through a combination of formative and summative 
instruments designed to evaluate knowledge, application, and critical thinking.

ASSESSMENT COMPONENT               WEIGHT      DUE DATE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Weekly Quizzes (8 x 2.5%)          20%         End of each week
Assessment 1: HR Strategy          15%         End of Week 2
Assessment 2: Case Study           15%         End of Week 4
Project Part 1: HR Audit           15%         End of Week 6
Project Part 2: HR Transformation  15%         End of Week 8
Final Examination                  20%         End of Week 8
----------------------------------------------------------------------
TOTAL                              100%

PASSING CRITERIA:
    - Overall score of 60% or higher.
    - No individual assessment component below 50%.
    - Completion of all weekly quizzes.
    - Participation in at least 75% of live sessions (if applicable).

                     CERTIFICATION DETAILS
================================================================================

CERTIFICATE NAME:
    HR Verse Professional Certificate in Human Resource Management

AWARDING BODY:
    HR Verse Academy, in partnership with recognized professional HR bodies.

CERTIFICATE VALIDITY:
    The certificate is valid for three (3) years from the date of issue.
    Holders are encouraged to pursue Continuing Professional Development 
    (CPD) activities to maintain currency.

CONTINUING EDUCATION CREDITS:
    This program qualifies for up to 60 Professional Development Credits 
    (PDCs) applicable toward SHRM-CP/SHRM-SCP recertification, subject to 
    individual verification with the Society for Human Resource Management.

DIGITAL CREDENTIALS:
    Successful participants receive a verifiable digital badge that can be 
    shared on LinkedIn, email signatures, and professional portfolios. The 
    digital badge is powered by blockchain-verified credential technology 
    to ensure authenticity.

CERTIFICATE REQUIREMENTS:
    To earn the HR Verse Professional Certificate, participants must:
    1. Achieve an overall program score of 60% or higher.
    2. Complete all required assessments and projects.
    3. Attend a minimum of 75% of scheduled live sessions.
    4. Adhere to the HR Verse Academic Integrity Policy throughout the 
       program.

PATHWAYS AFTER CERTIFICATION:
    Upon earning the HR Verse Professional Certificate, graduates may:
    - Pursue the HR Verse Advanced Diploma in Strategic HRM (Level 2).
    - Apply learning toward SHRM-CP, SHRM-SCP, PHR, or SPHR exam 
      preparation.
    - Access the HR Verse Alumni Network for mentorship, job postings, 
      and professional development events.
