CAPSTONE PROJECT PART 2: DIGITAL HR TRANSFORMATION & PEOPLE ANALYTICS

1. PROJECT BRIEF & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Capstone Project Part 2 focuses on modernizing legacy human resource operations through
digital transformation, AI-powered automation, and strategic people analytics.

Students assume the role of Chief People Officer (CPO) or VP of People Operations
tasked with leading a complete modernization of an organization's HR operating model.

Key Pillars of the Transformation:
1. HR Operating Model Evolution:
   - Migration from traditional transactional silos to Ulrich's Modern HR Model
     (HR Business Partners, Centers of Excellence, and Shared Services Portal).
2. Technology & AI Stack Architecture:
   - Evaluation, selection, and integration architecture of modern HRIS (Workday, BambooHR),
     ATS (Greenhouse, Lever), LMS/LXP (HR Verse), and AI-driven Talent Intelligence platforms.
   - Generative AI governance: Ethical AI policy for recruiting, candidate screening,
     performance summarization, and automated employee query resolution.
3. Advanced People Analytics Architecture:
   - Predictive attrition modeling, flight-risk indicators, organizational network analysis (ONA),
     and dynamic workforce planning simulations.
4. Change Management & Stakeholder Alignment:
   - Kotter's 8-Step Change Framework or Prosci ADKAR model application to guide adoption
     across executive leadership, managers, and frontline employees.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROJECT DELIVERABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deliverable 1: Digital HR Transformation Blueprint
- Architectural blueprint illustrating data flows between ATS, Core HRIS, LMS, Payroll, and Performance systems.
- Comprehensive AI Ethics & Employee Data Privacy Charter.

Deliverable 2: People Analytics Executive Dashboard Design
- Interactive dashboard prototype specification (or mockups) featuring:
  * Predictive attrition probability model by department and tenure.
  * Quality of Hire vs. Cost per Hire correlation matrix.
  * Pay Equity parity monitor with continuous compa-ratio variance tracking.
  * Skills taxonomy and capability readiness heatmaps.

Deliverable 3: Change Management Playbook & Business Case Pitch Deck
- Detailed ROI calculation justification showing net financial payback within 18 months.
- Stakeholder resistance analysis and engagement strategy.
- 12-month phased transition roadmap with milestone gates and post-implementation audit protocols.
