================================================================================
HR VERSE: PROFESSIONAL HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT CERTIFICATION
CAPSTONE PROJECT PART 1: COMPREHENSIVE ORGANIZATIONAL HR AUDIT
================================================================================

1. PROJECT BRIEF & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The HR Audit Capstone Project requires students to conduct an end-to-end audit of
an enterprise's human resource functions, evaluating legal compliance, operational
efficiency, strategic alignment, and data integrity.

Target Organization Options:
Option A: The learner's current or prior employer (anonymized data permitted).
Option B: "Global Retail Innovations (GRI)" - provided comprehensive synthetic enterprise dataset (850 employees, omnichannel consumer goods company).

Core Audit Functional Areas:
1. Legal & Regulatory Compliance (FLSA classifications, EEO-1 reporting, I-9 verification, statutory leaves, safety standards).
2. Talent Acquisition & Onboarding (Recruitment funnel efficiency, interview consistency, adverse impact compliance).
3. Total Rewards & Compensation Administration (Job descriptions, pay equity analysis, overtime compliance, benefits uptake).
4. Performance Management & Appraisals (Distribution curve analysis, calibration review, bias auditing).
5. HR Information Systems (HRIS) & People Data Privacy (Data access controls, GDPR/CCPA employee data rights, records retention).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROJECT DELIVERABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deliverable 1: HR Audit Scope & Methodology Charter
- Executive overview of audit objectives, timeline, stakeholder interviews, and data sampling methodology.

Deliverable 2: Quantitative Data Analytics & Compliance Audit Matrix
- Spreadsheets or analytical models detailing:
  * Equal Pay Audit (gender and ethnic pay parity regressions across job grades).
  * 4/5ths Rule adverse impact calculations across talent acquisition stages.
  * Overtime risk audit (exempt vs. non-exempt classification testing).

Deliverable 3: Final HR Audit Report & Executive Presentation
- 15-20 page comprehensive audit report with executive summary.
- Traffic-light risk matrix (High / Medium / Low risk classification).
- Prioritized Remediation Roadmap with immediate (30-day), intermediate (90-day), and systemic (1-year) corrective action plans.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. GRADING RUBRIC (100 TOTAL POINTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Methodological Rigor & Audit Thoroughness: 30 Points
- Data Analysis & Statistical Validity: 25 Points
- Legal & Regulatory Accuracy: 20 Points
- Strategic Recommendations & Risk Prioritization: 15 Points
- Executive Report Quality & Professional Formatting: 10 Points
