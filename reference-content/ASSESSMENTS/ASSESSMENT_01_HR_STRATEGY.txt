ASSESSMENT 01: STRATEGIC HR ROADMAP & WORKFORCE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. ASSESSMENT CONTEXT & SCENARIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Organization: "ApexLogix Technologies"
Company Profile: A 5-year-old enterprise SaaS scale-up that has grown from 45 to 380
employees across 3 geographic regions (US Headquarters, European Tech Hub, Latin America
Engineering Center). The company recently secured $65M Series C funding and is targeting
an initial public offering (IPO) within 24 months.

Current Critical Challenges:
1. Voluntary turnover in Engineering and Product departments reached 28% annualized.
2. Sourcing cycle time averages 74 days (Time to Fill), causing product release delays.
3. Total compensation bands lack systematic market benchmarking; employees report
   internal pay inequities and opaque promotion criteria.
4. Onboarding is fragmented and decentralized, leading to low 90-day engagement scores.
5. First-time managers lack training in performance coaching and feedback calibration.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CANDIDATE DELIVERABLE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Students must construct a 12-month comprehensive Strategic HR Roadmap (maximum 2,500 words)
subdivided into four mandatory strategic pillars:

Pillar 1: Talent Acquisition & Employer Branding Transformation
- Multi-channel sourcing architecture to compress Time to Fill from 74 to 42 days.
- Structured behavioral interviewing protocol with standardized rubrics (STAR).
- Employer Value Proposition (EVP) messaging strategy.

Pillar 2: Total Rewards & Compensation Engineering
- Step-by-step job evaluation plan using a Point-Factor methodology.
- Salary structure modeling: range spread, midpoints, and compa-ratio guardrails (0.80 - 1.20).
- Variable incentive redesign (short-term team bonuses + equity retention grants).

Pillar 3: Performance Management & Continuous Coaching System
- Transition from ad-hoc annual reviews to quarterly OKRs and bi-weekly 1-on-1s.
- 360-degree feedback framework for engineering and product leads.
- Calibrated performance evaluation rubric mitigating bias (Central Tendency, Halo/Horns).

Pillar 4: Learning, Leadership Development & Culture Architecture
- Structured 30-60-90 day onboarding framework for remote and hybrid teams.
- "New Manager Academy" curriculum applying adult learning theory (Knowles' Andragogy).
- Quantitative KPI scorecard tracking Quality of Hire, Retention, and Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. GRADING RUBRIC (100 TOTAL POINTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Strategic Alignment & Diagnostic Depth (25 Points)
   - Deep diagnostic linkage between business context (Series C to IPO) and HR interventions.
2. Technical HR Rigor & Methodological Soundness (25 Points)
   - Proper application of Point-Factor job evaluation, compa-ratio calculations, and BARS/OKR frameworks.
3. Implementation Feasibility & Change Management (25 Points)
   - Realistic 12-month phased roadmap with risk mitigation, budget considerations, and executive buy-in strategy.
4. Measurement, Analytics & KPI Dashboard (15 Points)
   - Concrete metrics (QoH, TTF, CPH, Compa-Ratio distribution, eNPS, retention).
5. Professional Communication & Structure (10 Points)
   - Executive-ready formatting, clarity, logical progression, and professional tone.
