1. CASE BACKGROUND: NOVAHEALTH INTEGRATED SYSTEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NovaHealth is a multi-facility healthcare network employing 2,400 clinical and
administrative personnel across seven regional hospitals and twelve outpatient clinics.
Following a major healthcare systems acquisition 18 months ago, the leadership team
attempted to harmonize operational cultures, wage scales, and shift rosters across
legacy and acquired facilities.

The Resulting Crisis:
- Registered Nurse (RN) turnover surged to 34.2% within the past 12 months.
- Agency/Locum staffing expenditure exploded by 310%, consuming $18.4M in temporary contract premiums.
- Clinical staff burnout metrics reached critical levels (78% reporting emotional exhaustion on the Maslach Burnout Inventory).
- A newly introduced biometric attendance system and rigid mandatory overtime policy led to an active union organizing petition.
- Exit interviews revealed four dominant themes: toxic supervisory behaviors, inconsistent shift scheduling, perceived wage suppression relative to regional competitors, and non-existent professional development pathways.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CASE STUDY QUESTIONS (SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Learners are required to submit an analytical diagnostic report (2,000 - 3,000 words)
answering the following core analytical questions:

Question 1: Root Cause & Systems Analysis
- Conduct a comprehensive Root Cause Analysis (Fishbone / Ishikawa or 5-Whys methodology) dissecting the interaction of compensation, leadership, operational integration, and organizational culture in driving RN attrition.

Question 2: Labor Relations & Risk Mitigation
- Formulate an immediate labor relations strategy addressing the unionization petition, ensuring legal compliance with national labor relations standards while establishing open, constructive employee communication forums.

Question 3: Total Rewards Restructuring & Locum Cost Abatement
- Construct a compensation strategy to eliminate the unsustainable $18.4M agency spend. Include shift differentials, retention bonuses, career ladder pay bands, and loan forgiveness programs.

Question 4: Wellbeing, Retention & Psychological Safety Architecture
- Design a nurse-centric wellness and psychological safety framework. Address scheduling flexibility (self-scheduling algorithms), peer support programs, and first-line clinical manager empathy training.

Question 5: Governance & Continuous Monitoring Dashboard
- Present an Executive HR Analytics Scorecard with leading and lagging indicators to monitor post-intervention stabilization over 6, 12, and 18 months.
