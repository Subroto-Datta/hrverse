================================================================================
                         HR VERSE
          PROFESSIONAL HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM
                       COURSE ROADMAP
                   WEEK-BY-WEEK LEARNING GUIDE
================================================================================

This roadmap provides a detailed week-by-week breakdown of the HR Verse 
Professional Human Resource Management Program. Each week includes module 
topics, learning objectives, estimated study hours, and required deliverables.

================================================================================
                    WEEK 1: FOUNDATIONS OF HRM & TALENT ACQUISITION
                    Dates: Program Start – End of Week 1
                    Estimated Total Effort: 15 hours
================================================================================

MODULE 01: INTRODUCTION TO HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT (5 hours)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Topics Covered:
    1.1  Defining Human Resource Management
    1.2  Historical Evolution of HR: From Personnel Administration to 
         Strategic HRM
    1.3  Core HR Functions: Staffing, Development, Compensation, Safety, 
         Employee Relations
    1.4  The HR Professional's Role in Modern Organizations
    1.5  HR as a Strategic Business Partner
    1.6  Ethical Foundations of HR Practice
    1.7  Key HR Professional Bodies: SHRM, CIPD, HRCI
    1.8  HR Competency Models and Frameworks

Learning Objectives:
    - Define HRM and articulate its strategic importance.
    - Trace the evolution of HR from administrative function to strategic 
      partner.
    - Identify core HR functions and how they interconnect.
    - Explain the ethical responsibilities of HR professionals.

Activities:
    - Read Module 01 learning materials (90 minutes)
    - Watch video lectures on HR evolution (60 minutes)
    - Discussion forum: "What makes HR strategic?" (30 minutes)
    - Self-assessment exercise: HR function mapping (60 minutes)

MODULE 02: HR PLANNING & JOB ANALYSIS (5 hours)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Topics Covered:
    2.1  Strategic Workforce Planning
    2.2  Labor Demand Forecasting Methods (Trend Analysis, Ratio Analysis, 
         Regression Analysis)
    2.3  Labor Supply Analysis (Internal and External)
    2.4  Succession Planning
    2.5  Job Analysis: Purpose and Methods (Observation, Interviews, 
         Questionnaires, PAQ, O*NET)
    2.6  Writing Job Descriptions
    2.7  Developing Person Specifications and Competency Profiles
    2.8  The Relationship Between Job Analysis and Other HR Functions

Learning Objectives:
    - Explain the workforce planning process and its strategic significance.
    - Apply demand and supply forecasting techniques.
    - Conduct job analysis using multiple methods.
    - Write clear, accurate job descriptions and person specifications.

Activities:
    - Read Module 02 learning materials (90 minutes)
    - Case exercise: Workforce demand forecasting for a retail chain 
      (60 minutes)
    - Practical exercise: Write a job description for an HR Coordinator 
      (60 minutes)
    - Quiz preparation review (30 minutes)

MODULE 03: RECRUITMENT & SELECTION (5 hours)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Topics Covered:
    3.1  The Recruitment Process: Planning to Offer
    3.2  Internal vs. External Recruitment
    3.3  Sourcing Strategies: Job Boards, Social Media, Employee Referrals, 
         Recruitment Agencies, Campus Recruiting
    3.4  Employer Branding and the Employee Value Proposition (EVP)
    3.5  Selection Methods: Application Screening, Psychometric Testing, 
         Work Samples, Assessment Centers
    3.6  Interviewing Techniques: Structured, Behavioral (STAR), 
         Situational, Panel
    3.7  Making the Hiring Decision: Validity, Reliability, and Bias 
         Reduction
    3.8  Onboarding and New Employee Integration
    3.9  Recruitment Metrics: Time to Fill, Cost per Hire, Quality of Hire

Learning Objectives:
    - Design an end-to-end recruitment process.
    - Evaluate sourcing strategies for effectiveness and reach.
    - Conduct structured interviews using behavioral techniques.
    - Develop an onboarding program that accelerates new hire productivity.

Activities:
    - Read Module 03 learning materials (90 minutes)
    - Video case study: Recruitment at a Fortune 500 company (45 minutes)
    - Role-play exercise: Conduct a structured behavioral interview 
      (60 minutes)
    - Group discussion: Reducing bias in selection (30 minutes)

WEEK 1 DELIVERABLES:
    ✓ Complete Quiz: Week 1 Knowledge Check (20 multiple-choice questions)
    ✓ Submit discussion forum post: "What makes HR strategic?"
    ✓ Complete practical exercise: Job description writing

    6.7  Evaluating Training Effectiveness: Kirkpatrick's Four Levels,

    6.8  Career Development and Career Pathing
    6.9  Talent Pipelines and High-Potential Programs
    6.10 Learning Management Systems (LMS) and Learning Technologies

Learning Objectives:
    - Conduct a training needs analysis at organizational, task, and

    - Design a training program using the ADDIE model.
    - Evaluate training ROI using Kirkpatrick and Phillips frameworks.
    - Create career development pathways for employees.

Activities:
    - Read Module 06 learning materials (90 minutes)
    - Practical exercise: Conduct a training needs analysis for a
