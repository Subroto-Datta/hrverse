1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & LEARNING OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Talent Acquisition is no longer merely an administrative recruiting function; it is
a strategic differentiator in the modern knowledge-driven economy. Organizations
compete vigorously for top-tier capability, making the design and execution of an
effective, equitable, and data-driven recruitment and selection system paramount.

By the conclusion of this module, learners will be able to:
1. Differentiate between transactional recruiting and strategic talent acquisition.
2. Formulate multi-channel sourcing strategies including employee referrals, direct
   executive sourcing, digital platforms, and campus talent pipelines.
3. Apply structured behavioral and competency-based interview methodologies (STAR/CAR).
4. Implement psychometric, cognitive, and assessment center evaluation frameworks.
5. Calculate and optimize core Talent Acquisition metrics: Quality of Hire (QoH),
   Time to Fill (TTF), Cost per Hire (CPH), and Offer Acceptance Rate (OAR).
6. Build an exceptional candidate experience and structured 30-60-90 day onboarding
   architecture that accelerates time-to-productivity and drives long-term retention.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STRATEGIC TALENT ACQUISITION FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Talent acquisition encompasses employer branding, future workforce forecasting,
candidate relationship management (CRM), and talent pipelining before vacancies arise.

Key Pillars of Modern Talent Acquisition:
A. Employer Value Proposition (EVP):
   The holistic set of offerings (monetary compensation, benefits, career growth,
   company mission, workplace culture, and flexibility) conveyed to prospective talent.
B. Proactive Talent Pipelining:
   Engaging passive candidates through content marketing, talent communities, and
   continuous industry networking ahead of requisition approvals.
C. Candidate Experience (CX):
   The sum of all touchpoints between a prospective hire and the enterprise,
   from job advertisement readability to prompt interview feedback and empathetic rejection notices.

3. CANDIDATE SOURCING STRATEGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Inbound Sourcing:
   - Optimized Careers Portals: Mobile-first responsive career pages with authentic employee stories.
   - Job Board Aggregators: Strategic placement on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and niche trade boards.
   - Search Engine Optimization (SEO) for Job Posts: Schema.org job posting markup.

2. Outbound & Passive Sourcing:
   - Boolean and Semantic Search across professional repositories (GitHub for engineering, Behance for designers).
   - Direct Headhunting for executive and critical leadership positions.
   - Alumni and Silver Medalist Networks: Re-engaging runner-up candidates from previous searches.

3. Internal Mobility & Referral Systems:
   - Employee Referral Programs (ERP): Structuring incentives (monetary and recognition) while mitigating homophily risks.
   - Internal Talent Marketplaces: Enabling existing employees to apply for cross-functional assignments, transfers, and promotions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SELECTION METHODOLOGIES & ASSESSMENT DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The selection process must maximize predictive validity while minimizing adverse impact
and cognitive bias.

Predictive Validity of Selection Instruments (Schmidt & Hunter Meta-Analysis Benchmark):
- Work Sample Tests: ~0.54 predictive validity coefficient.
- General Mental Ability (GMA) / Cognitive Tests: ~0.51.
- Structured Behavioral Interviews: ~0.51.
- Unstructured Interviews: ~0.38 (High susceptibility to halo effect, primacy/recency bias).
- Reference Checks: ~0.26.
- Graphology / Personality inventories (standalone): ~0.10 - 0.22.

The Gold Standard: Multi-Hurdle Evaluation Architecture:
- Stage 1: Asynchronous Resume Screening & Qualification Assessment (AI-assisted with bias auditing).
- Stage 2: Initial Video/Phone Screening (Recruiter alignment on expectations, compensation, logistical fit).
- Stage 3: Technical / Competency Assessment or Work Sample (Blind review when applicable).
- Stage 4: Structured Competency & Behavioral Interview (Panel with standardized rubrics).
- Stage 5: Values & Team Collaboration Alignment.
- Stage 6: Formal Background & Credential Verification.

Behavioral Interviewing Technique (STAR Method):
- Situation: Context of the past event.
- Task: Specific challenge or objective the candidate was assigned.
- Action: Discrete personal actions taken by the candidate.
- Result: Measurable outcome, quantifiable metrics, and lessons learned.

5. OFFER MANAGEMENT & NEW HIRE ONBOARDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Offer Formulation:
   - Total rewards alignment against benchmark market percentiles (P50, P75).
   - Total package presentation: Base salary, annual variable incentives (STI),
     equity grants (RSUs/Options), sign-on bonuses, relocation, and healthcare.
   - Mitigating pre-start attrition: Continuous engagement between offer acceptance
     and Day 1 ("Keep Warm" cadences).

B. The 30-60-90 Day Structured Onboarding Model:
   - Days 1-30 (Learn & Assimilate): Company orientation, compliance completions,
     buddy pairing, tooling access, cultural immersion, and shadowing.
   - Days 31-60 (Contribute & Collaborate): Independent task execution, cross-functional
     stakeholder introduction, initial sprint contributions, first check-in review.
   - Days 61-90 (Accelerate & Optimize): Full workload autonomy, stretch project assignment,
     formal 90-day performance review and long-term goal setting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CORE TALENT ACQUISITION METRICS & ANALYTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Time to Fill (TTF): Calendar days from job requisition approval to candidate offer acceptance.
- Time to Hire (TTH): Calendar days from candidate application to offer acceptance.
- Cost per Hire (CPH): (Total Internal Recruiting Costs + External Costs) / Total Hires.
- Quality of Hire (QoH): Composite index combining 90-day retention, 1-year performance appraisal rating, and hiring manager satisfaction score.
- Sourcing Channel Effectiveness: Conversion rates per channel through the funnel stages.
- Offer Acceptance Rate (OAR): Accepted Offers / Total Offers Extended * 100%.
